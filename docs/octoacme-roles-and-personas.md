# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Strategic Sponsor

### Role Summary
Strategic Sponsors are executive stakeholders who provide high-level direction, budget approval, and organizational alignment. They champion project initiatives at the executive level and remove organizational barriers to success.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and business rationale for projects
- Approve budgets and resource allocations
- Ensure organizational alignment with company strategy
- Champion project initiatives at the executive level
- Review milestone progress and provide executive-level guidance
- Approve major scope changes and trade-offs
- Escalate and resolve organizational obstacles

### Goals
- Ensure project alignment with organizational strategy
- Maximize return on investment (ROI)
- Remove blockers and maintain executive sponsorship
- Support successful project delivery and adoption

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Approves major decisions, reviews milestone progress, provides budget authority
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on strategic goals and business case, reviews roadmap prioritization
- **Developers**: Removes organizational barriers, advocates for project support across departments

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive steering meetings
- Milestone approval reviews
- Budget and resource allocation decisions
- Escalation of critical issues

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide deep technical expertise, guide architectural decisions, and manage technical debt. They mentor team members on best practices and ensure technical feasibility of project commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct technical feasibility assessments
- Design and review system architecture
- Provide technical guidance on implementation approaches
- Identify and manage technical debt and risks
- Mentor developers on best practices and code quality
- Review complex technical designs and pull requests
- Advise on technology choices and tools

### Goals
- Ensure technical soundness and maintainability
- Reduce technical risk and debt
- Elevate team technical capabilities
- Optimize performance and scalability

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Advises on technical constraints, estimates, and scheduling impacts
- **Developers**: Mentors team, reviews complex designs, provides technical guidance
- **Product Manager**: Advises on technical feasibility of features and trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and pull request feedback
- Technical risk discussions with Project Manager
- Knowledge sharing sessions and mentoring

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, clarify, and document requirements from stakeholders. They bridge communication between business needs and technical solutions, ensuring the team understands and can deliver what users truly need.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Identify requirements gaps and clarifications needed
- Translate business needs into technical acceptance criteria
- Validate that solutions meet stakeholder requirements
- Document processes and workflows
- Facilitate discussions between business and technical teams
- Support user research and validation activities

### Goals
- Ensure accurate understanding of business needs
- Reduce requirements defects and rework
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction
- Enable effective product delivery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Manager**: Works with PM on requirements, provides stakeholder feedback, supports validation
- **Project Manager**: Documents requirements, identifies scope impacts, tracks requirement changes
- **Developers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria, answers implementation questions, validates solutions

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specification reviews
- Stakeholder interviews and discovery sessions
- Clarification discussions with development team
- Acceptance criteria and definition of done

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads establish quality standards, define testing strategies, and ensure products meet acceptance criteria before release. They advocate for quality and work closely with teams to minimize defects and ensure user satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality metrics
- Establish quality standards and acceptance criteria validation
- Plan and oversee testing activities
- Identify, track, and prioritize quality issues
- Advise on automation and testing best practices
- Conduct release readiness reviews
- Support continuous improvement of quality processes

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality and reliability
- Minimize production defects and customer impact
- Reduce time to quality
- Support continuous quality improvement

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Advises on release readiness, quality metrics, testing schedule impacts
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test strategy, reviews quality practices, identifies edge cases
- **Product Manager**: Validates acceptance criteria, supports user acceptance testing, confirms quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and quality metrics discussions
- Defect reviews and prioritization
- Release readiness assessments
- Quality metrics reporting and trend analysis

---

## Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Risk Officers proactively identify risks, develop mitigation strategies, and track risk metrics throughout project execution. They work across teams to assess threats and opportunities, supporting the project manager in risk management.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project risks and opportunities
- Develop risk mitigation and contingency plans
- Maintain risk register and track risk metrics
- Escalate critical risks to project leadership
- Conduct risk review sessions with project teams
- Monitor risk indicators and trend analysis
- Support organizational learning from past incidents

### Goals
- Proactively identify and mitigate project risks
- Reduce unplanned disruptions and escalations
- Enable informed decision-making about risk trade-offs
- Support project success through risk management

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Partners on risk identification, mitigation planning, and escalation decisions
- **Technical Lead**: Assesses technical risks and proposes mitigations, reviews architectural risks
- **Developers**: Identifies implementation risks, proposes technical mitigations, supports risk monitoring

### Typical Communication
- Risk identification and assessment discussions
- Risk mitigation planning sessions
- Risk register reviews and updates
- Executive escalation of critical risks
- Post-project risk reviews and lessons learned
